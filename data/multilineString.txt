What's the difference between SugarKubes and LIGIT?
SugarKubes is for when you want to keep the code private. When someone makes a purchase they get added as a collaborator to the private repo.
LIGIT is the opposite.
You keep the code open source and monetize via a license. So someone will purchase a license for your project and then be able to use it however they need to.
